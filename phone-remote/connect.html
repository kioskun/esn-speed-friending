Connect your phone

1. Put the phone and this laptop on the same phone hotspot.
2. Click Open the show, move it to the projector and press F11. Click once on the show so sound can play.
3. Click Show QR and scan it with the phone camera. Do this before guests arrive or while the projector shows something else: the QR holds the remote's code, which is new every time the launcher starts. It hides itself after 60 seconds.

Open the show   Checking the show…

Phone can't open it? Use a phone hotspot (venue Wi-Fi often blocks this), and run phone-remote\allow-phone-in-firewall.bat once on this laptop. A new key is made every time the launcher starts, so after a restart scan the QR again. Keyboard and TeamViewer keep working as normal. Settings (Esc) are saved separately for this address: set them in the show opened from this page. Don't click inside the black launcher window, because that can pause it.
