Connect your phone

1. Put the phone and this laptop on the same Wi-Fi or phone hotspot.
2. Scan the QR with the phone camera. The remote opens in the phone browser; keep that page open.
3. Open the show (button below), move it to the projector and press F11. Click once on the show so sound can play.

Open the show

Phone can't open it? Use a phone hotspot (venue Wi-Fi often blocks this), and allow Windows PowerShell in the Windows Firewall prompt (tick Private and Public). Settings (Esc) are saved separately for this address: set them here, in the show opened from this page. Don't click inside the black launcher window, because that can pause it.
