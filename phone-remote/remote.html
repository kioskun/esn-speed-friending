Quick start

  1. On the laptop: open the show with Open the show on the Connect page (not the HTML file), move it to the projector, press F11, and click it once so sound can play.
  2. ⚙ Settings here: city, venue, date, time and the number of tables. The Welcome screen updates straight away.
  3. Hand out the badges, then use the big button. It always says what it will do next (Next screen, Start round, Pause…).
  4. On Let's mix it up! the big button does the warm-up move, then round 1 starts.
  5. Rotate now, Break, Welcome and Reset need two taps. Lock keys stops accidental key presses on the laptop.
  6. The slider sets the volume live. Mute silences everything.

The keyboard and TeamViewer keep working at the same time. If this page says "Old code", scan the QR on the laptop again (it changes every time the launcher starts).
